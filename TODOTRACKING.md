- the core functionality for money tracking needs to be fixed and redesigned. 
- there are Corner cases were some values are missed in calculations for example when user fixes his partial share and total sum of partial shares is higher than total of particular bill, then some of that partial share for specific user is not reflected in calculated amount that he owns to others. Looks like it's calculated up to Bill's total and not Beyond. 
- due to above let's make statements and priorities. 

1. all bills have theirs total value. Sum of these values is presented on main screen in the top and also in Settings screen as "Total tracked". this value is for information purpose only and is not main indicator of owed money.
2. every meal has parts: A) bill total. this is main cost of meal, it is how much bill paying person had to pay for it in the restaurant when covering his and rest of participants' expenses; B) partial share in one bill associated to paying person. this is amount that person who covered the bill in restaurant declared (fixed or got calculated automatically) that his part of meal costed. amount he spent. doesn't need to be tracked, same person spends and pays. C) partial shares of other participants. **THIS IS MOST IMPORTANT PART**: these are shares in one bill that other participants have spent and are owed to bill paying person. **THIS NEEDS TO BE TRACKED CAREFULY**. this get into later calculations of "who owns whom". D) diff-field. shown next to paying person. this is field which informs participants of single bill, when they all start to declare their spending (fix it), of how much still they owe (need to decide and still modify shares) to paying person. it has meaning for single bill, information only. it is calculated by finding difference between bill total (declared in field on top of meal card) and sum of all partial shares of users (every participant of meal, including paying person).  
3. in the settings there are sections related to who Owns whom and all money calculation. they should be calculated based on point 2C described above. this is the main tracking mechanism.
